when pirates kidnapped julius caesar, they had no idea they just signed their own death warrants.

​instead of begging for his life, caesar literally laughed in their faces, insulted them for asking for such a low ransom, and demanded they double it because he was "worth way more."

​he spent the next 38 days treating the pirates like his personal servants, writing poetry, and casually reminding them that he’d have them all executed the moment he was free. they thought he was joking.
​he wasn't.

​the second the ransom was paid, caesar raised a private fleet, tracked them down, took his money back, and crucified every single one of them. talk about the ultimate "call an ambulance, but not for me" moment in ancient history.
